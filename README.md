## 📖 About The Repository

The generation of electrical energy is the foundation of power engineering. This repository consolidates conceptual notes, circuit diagrams, layout schemes, formulas, and previous exam questions into one organized repository.

Whether you are preparing for semester examinations, competitive exams (such as SSC JE, State AE/JE, RRB JE), or technical viva interviews, this repo is built to make power generation intuitive and accessible.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience

- **Diploma in Electrical Engineering** (State Technical Boards: BTE, MSBTE, SBTE, etc.)
- **B.Tech / B.E. Electrical Engineering** students (Initial power system courses)
- **Junior Engineer (JE) Aspirants** (SSC JE, State Electricity Boards, Metro Rail)
- Anyone interested in conventional and renewable energy technologies.

---

## 📚 Key Topics Covered

### 1. Conventional Power Generation
* **Thermal Power Plants (Coal/Gas):**
  * Rankine cycle overview
  * Major components: Boilers, Economizers, Superheaters, Turbines, Condensers, Cooling Towers
  * Ash handling and coal handling plants
* **Hydroelectric Power Plants:**
  * Site selection & classifications (High, Medium, Low head)
  * Hydrology terms: Run-off, Catchment area, Hydrograph, Flow duration curve
  * Turbines: Pelton Wheel, Francis, Kaplan
* **Nuclear Power Plants:**
  * Nuclear fission mechanism and chain reactions
  * Core components: Reactor core, Moderators, Control rods, Coolants, Shielding
  * Reactor types: PWR, BWR, CANDU

### 2. Renewable & Non-Conventional Sources
* **Solar Energy:** PV cell characteristics, solar thermal collectors, grid-tied vs. off-grid systems
* **Wind Energy:** Betz limit, Horizontal vs. Vertical axis wind turbines (HAWT/VAWT)
* **Biomass & Bio-gas Plants**
* **Geothermal & Tidal Energy Basics**

### 3. Economics of Power Generation
* Load curves, Load duration curves, and Demand factors
* Diversity factor, Plant capacity factor, Plant use factor
* Tariff structures (Flat rate, Two-part, Maximum demand tariff)
* Cost comparison between different plant types
